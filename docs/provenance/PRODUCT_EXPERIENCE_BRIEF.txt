PRODUCT STORY
Most people want crypto exposure, but they don't want to watch charts, read every headline, swap tokens, and rebalance by hand.
Wave lets a user put $100 into one basket. The money is split automatically. Wave then watches market and news risk and moves the basket within hard limits.

The target is to reduce exposure during risk events, not to keep every portfolio green. Never promise profits, safety, or crash protection.

WHAT THE USER SHOULD SEE
1. Choose a basket card.
2. Put in $100.
3. See the money split across assets through real swaps.
4. See holdings, NAV, and each asset's weight.
5. See a risk event and an AI decision card.
6. See the portfolio move through bounded rebalances, such as BTC 50% to 35% to 20%, using as many valid steps as the limits require.
7. See every swap and transaction.
8. Withdraw to USDC or in kind.
