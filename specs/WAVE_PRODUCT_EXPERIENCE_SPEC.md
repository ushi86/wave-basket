## Problem Statement

Wave has basket contracts, a local Uniswap v4 lifecycle, a deterministic CRE market strategy, privacy routing and a functioning dashboard. It does not yet demonstrate the complete consumer journey of choosing among three genuine baskets, putting in $100, seeing actual allocation and NAV, understanding a risk decision, watching bounded rebalances, and withdrawing.

The product must make Uniswap execution, Chainlink valuation and automation, and Anon-RPC private reads understandable through the user's actions. Technical scaffolding, an unverified Sepolia deployment, static cards or invented decisions cannot satisfy that need. The owner also needs phase-by-phase review and authentic submission evidence without weakening contract limits or overstating what works.

## Solution

Build a card-based Wave Protocol experience around “Token baskets, in balance.” Users choose Growth, Balanced or Yield, deposit 100 test settlement tokens, inspect real swaps, holdings and NAV, understand sourced risk decisions, see bounded allocation changes, and exit to settlement tokens or in kind.

Keep the three existing narrative pillars: fair value through Uniswap v4 and NavGuardHook; target allocation through Chainlink feeds and CRE; private visibility through Anon-RPC. Explain baskets as collateralized onchain tokens comparable to fund/index allocations, without calling them ETFs or making legal, returns, protection or production-readiness claims. The goal of the strategy is to reduce exposure during risk events; it does not ensure positive returns or prevent losses.

Work through Phases 0–10 below, stopping after every phase for the owner to say continue. Begin with a read-only assessment, including explicit missing inputs and deployment blockers. Reuse working modules and tests. Separate confirmed live state, local fixtures, simulated yield and historical replay throughout the experience and evidence.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Use the current domain.** Preserve basket/share token, vault, factory, registry, settlement asset, NAV, NAV per share, actual/target weights, high-water mark, report, forwarder, receiver and rebalance history terminology. Explain the conceptual Vault, Allocation and Settlement layers through existing modules rather than a contract rewrite. No glossary or ADR was found in the inspected checkout; do not invent one as established architecture.
- **Origin material.** The supplied 15-page whitepaper was found at the repository root, read completely, and preserved under the documentation directory. Carry forward its abstraction, NAV/share mechanics, three conceptual layers and manual/algorithmic product modes. Current implementation and owner constraints supersede aspirational claims or incompatible illustrative economics in that document. Wave is the only public product name. Manual and algorithmic management are product modes; an AI badge requires a real approved AI step.
- **Freeze core fund contracts.** Do not change WaveVault or WaveVaultDeployer, including the deployer definition colocated with the factory. If a proven requirement cannot work without changing them, stop and present the failing behavior and proposed change. New onchain behavior belongs in separate necessary contracts. Do not redeploy existing Sepolia contracts or move funds without explicit owner approval.
- **Preserve invariants.** Keep a maximum 50% asset weight, 20% turnover, 1% per-swap and aggregate NAV-loss protection, five-minute rebalance interval and ten-minute manual-proposal timelock. Existing positive-weight, total-weight and asset-count rules still apply. Turnover uses actual holdings including residual cash; a fifteen-point target change is not automatically a valid step after market drift. Existing manual target activation does not itself trade holdings; UI text must reflect that behavior.
- **Exit and fee policy.** Retain both exit paths, the existing withdrawal fee and high-water-mark performance-fee behavior. Healthy-feed exits crystallize applicable creator fees. Stale-feed in-kind exits remain immediate and full net pro-rata after the existing withdrawal fee, with no withheld reserve. Disclose that they may avoid uncrystallized creator fees. No deferred stale-exit reserve is implemented; a future design requires expiry. “No lock-in” does not remove manual-strategy timing rules or ordinary transaction conditions.
- **Consumer shell.** Reuse the existing React/viem model, navigation, basket reads and transaction flow. Organize Explore, basket/position detail, Portfolio, Create and Settings/Demo Setup around cards. Existing Explore, Portfolio, Create, Privacy and Faucet pages are starting points, not missing features to rebuild blindly. Move setup/faucet prominence out of the primary journey. Keep loading, stale, unavailable, pending, failed, skipped and confirmed states distinct and accessible.
- **Card plan.** Basket cards contain purpose, relative risk, assets, actual NAV and fee summary; Deposit shows expected/confirmed split; Allocation compares actual/target; Portfolio shows owned shares and value; Swap Activity shows executed swaps and fees; AI Decision shows source and decision provenance; Timeline shows proposal/delivery/execution and cooldown; Yield shows NAV per share/history/reserve status; Price vs NAV shows the hooked pool; Privacy shows route and measurement; Withdraw shows fee/net exit choices; Create uses existing factory rules. No card hard-codes weights, NAV, swaps, decisions or transaction outcomes as live state. Unavailable data remains unavailable.
- **Three real baskets.** Growth uses risk assets such as test WETH/WBTC/LINK; Balanced includes settlement exposure; Yield includes a backed test yield asset, settlement and smaller risk exposure. Final valid weights are not yet selected and must fit current registry/vault rules. Relative risk categories are descriptions, not guarantees. A card becomes an active product only with a verified contract, registered assets and usable liquidity. Preserve any existing basket; do not infer that a flagship can be renamed or replaced without approval.
- **CRE coverage.** The existing deterministic workflow reads an external 24-hour market-change source and inspects at most the first three factory entries. Check actual ordering before claiming Growth/Balanced/Yield coverage. If an older flagship occupies a slot, use explicit basket selection or bounded iteration that reaches all three; do not silently omit one. Keep any coverage change outside frozen fund contracts and test it with extra registered baskets.
- **Yield integration.** Add a separate MockYieldToken and compatible yield-rate/valuation adapter only if required. Use the existing registry interface and validate decimals, timestamps, answer ranges, rounds and liquidity. Specify a reserve ledger with conservation between funded reserves, accrued liabilities and redeemable backing; fresh minting or feed-price growth alone cannot create backing. Accrual must be bounded by the funded reserve and stop at exhaustion. Test valuation and redemption under that rule before registration. Do not present a rate feed as a real Chainlink feed unless it is one. Keep “Simulated yield on Sepolia” visible and make NAV per share the headline; no advertised real yield or invented historical curve.
- **Deposit and valuation.** “Put in $100” means 100 test USDC units in the demo, not receipt of real dollars or a guaranteed dollar peg. Show Chainlink valuation and settlement units accurately. NAV per share derives from total valued holdings and total supply with existing decimal/seed conventions. Show expected allocation before wallet approval, actual v4 swap receipts and confirmed shares afterward. Reuse minimum-output and fee-aware previews; never substitute target weights for actual holdings.
- **Risk input and decision contract.** Extend the existing strategy using real external market/news observations. Preserve source URL/identity, source/observation time, content hash, schema version, risk level, bounded score and short reason. Treat source text as untrusted data. Validate missing fields, finite ranges, timestamps, freshness and timeouts. On any required source or AI failure, malformed output or timeout, hold the current allocation and record why; send no new rebalance report. A hold is not a successful rebalance. Do not relabel the existing momentum strategy as news-aware AI.
- **LLM prerequisite.** Ask the owner which provider to use before adding an LLM dependency or requiring its credentials. The owner configures credentials privately through the supported secret mechanism; never request their value in chat, source or browser configuration. Without that prerequisite, keep AI integration blocked and describe existing algorithmic decisions honestly. No account, paid service or unlisted third-party tool installation without approval.
- **CRE authority and records.** Retain the existing report payload and receiver freshness, nonce, forwarder and vault authentication. AI output is a proposal, not signing authority. Derive valid steps from actual state and recompute after each execution and cooldown. Preserve observation/decision provenance, proposal, report, forwarder transaction, receiver outcome and resulting basket event/state. Revalidate the workflow's current two-million-gas write setting under Sepolia's gas rules before broadcast; do not confuse a higher justified gas allowance with relaxed portfolio bounds. A real CLI simulation with broadcast and successful receiver execution is required for live automation evidence.
- **Replay semantics.** Use real saved historical prices and news with identities and observation/publication times. Keep replay records separate from live decisions and persist the visible REPLAY label through every card and animation. No invented headlines, future-information leakage, backdated live reports or forged historical Sepolia receipts. Recompute each step under all allocation, turnover, interval, execution and timelock rules that apply. A 50%→35%→20% example is illustrative; the required number of valid steps follows actual state. Include fees, costs and assumptions in comparisons and show gaps rather than fabricated observations. A replay on current testnet contracts remains a replay.
- **Uniswap and hook views.** All funded deposit, settlement-exit and rebalance swaps use the existing v4 adapter and intended official Sepolia PoolManager. In-kind exit transfers assets without inventing swaps. Only the approved flagship key uses NavGuardHook; other baskets must not imply they have a hooked pool. Show actual configured fee rules and executed fees, not a universal guarantee of convergence. New hooked pools require a separate design consistent with the existing single-approved-key restriction; they are not silently added.
- **Privacy continuity.** Retain immutable Nox 0.4.0 and its known worker-hash check. Do not switch to mutable 0.4.1 automatically. A failed full Sepolia session stops measurement-dependent work and presents actual errors/options. App reads remain fail-closed with no silent public fallback. Retain actual per-method attempts, successes, failures, startup and explicit routing exceptions; onchain balances and transactions remain public. Wallet, bootstrap and off-browser CRE traffic are separately disclosed.
- **Deployment continuity.** The inspected release has no verified successful Wave Sepolia deployment. The prior creation transaction reverted at block 12,076,125; owner nonce became 1. The later native Foundry 1.8.3 Amsterdam fork run passed all 42 planned operations with 175,154,050 gas, but it is a dry run. Full RPC batch validation remains incomplete; generated addresses remain predictions. Recovery-command preparation and fresh receipt/nonce/feed/fee checks are prerequisites to any owner broadcast. Preserve failed artifacts and sensitive cache locally, keep secrets out of Git and never blindly resume the nonce-0 plan. PublicNode is the explicitly authorized broadcaster; Tenderly remains accountless/read-only. Their diversity does not prove fully independent infrastructure.
- **Phase contract.** Each implemented phase yields reviewable visible behavior, relevant tests, typecheck/build results, a small normal commit/push, screenshots for UI work, a working/mock/replay/unverified inventory and an update to the product-progress record. Run the existing stale-exit, fee, hook, receiver and safety-limit regressions after every phase. Phase 0 changes no product code: its visible deliverable is a report; existing checks may run read-only and missing tooling is disclosed. Stop at each phase boundary and wait for “continue.” No setup or implement-spec skill is used for this project. The owner has already authorized Phase 1 after Phase 0; stop after Phase 1.
- **Evidence and submission.** Preserve real canonical receipt/log/state checks, immutable revision/line links, CRE CLI provenance, full browser privacy measurements and required feedback/attribution/privacy documents. Validation reads artifacts and fails instead of creating missing evidence. A successful forwarder transaction alone does not prove receiver execution. The owner performs signatures, logins, funding, feedback-form submission, recording, final partner selection and submission. Do not claim prize eligibility solely because integrations are present.

## Testing Decisions

- **Owner-confirmed primary seam:** exercise the existing public basket lifecycle through the real user flow for each of Growth, Balanced and Yield: choose, put in 100 test USDC, confirm actual split, inspect NAV/holdings, execute a bounded rebalance and complete both withdrawal paths. Reuse the public lifecycle and real local v4 integration tests, adding browser-level orchestration where needed rather than separate test APIs or component mocks for every card.
- **Good tests:** assert user-visible state, onchain balances/supply, reserve conservation, NAV, accepted/rejected operations, events, receipts, rendered provenance and actual routing. Avoid implementation-helper, SDK-order and self-derived snapshot assertions. Deterministic fixtures are legitimate test inputs, clearly distinguished from live evidence.
- **Real card state:** vary underlying holdings, prices, fee accrual, CRE outcome and transaction status; assert cards reflect those variations. A failed/pending receipt cannot render confirmed success. Confirmed weights and displayed swaps must match contract/event data, including a correct hold with no rebalance event. Test empty/unconfigured/unavailable states rather than hard-coded demo fallbacks.
- **Reserve-backed Yield:** fund a real test reserve, accrue funded value, check NAV/share value and redemption backing, then exhaust the reserve. Assert accrual stops, liabilities never exceed backing, no repeated settlement double counts yield, and the UI never grows value from an unfunded rate. Test registry compatibility, decimals, unhealthy valuation and both exits through the public lifecycle without changing frozen fund contracts.
- **Source/AI hold:** extend strategy/workflow tests for HTTP errors, stale/missing observations, invalid schema, non-finite/out-of-range score, malformed AI output and timeouts. Each must leave current allocations unchanged and emit no new rebalance report. Preserve provenance and distinguish hold/skip/rejection from execution. Verify all three intended baskets are reachable when extra baskets exist.
- **Multi-step replay:** prove a 50%→20% exposure move uses as many valid steps as actual holdings and unchanged limits require. Verify sums, per-asset cap, turnover/cash-leg accounting, cooldown, NAV-loss bounds, ordering and applicable timelock at every step. Verify no future source information is consumed. The UI must display REPLAY continuously; historical comparisons must include disclosed costs and assumptions.
- **Unchanged regressions, every phase:** keep stale-feed full net pro-rata in-kind exits with no reserve, healthy-exit creator fee consistency, high-water-mark accounting, fee-aware net previews, single approved hook pool, forwarder authentication, stale/future/replayed/non-sequential report rejection and all portfolio safety-limit tests. Existing tests change only when an explicitly approved behavior change demands it; this spec does not approve changes to frozen contracts.
- **Private reads:** reuse transport/meter tests and add a full browser session. Worker failure must block app reads, preserve zero silent direct fallback, count batch members correctly and report measured startup and coverage even when targets are missed. Keep the approved worker pin unchanged.
- **Live acceptance:** separately require owner-authorized canonical Sepolia transactions for each claimed basket and lifecycle, actual CRE CLI broadcast plus receiver execution, hook fee evidence for the approved pool and a full Anon-RPC browser session. Compilation, local tests and replay are never substitutes.
- **Evidence rejection:** retain existing evidence-checker negatives and reject local/mock/dry-run/replayed results misrepresented as live Sepolia activity. Fail for missing public repo/revision line links, Sepolia hashes, CRE simulation log, Anon-RPC measurements or FEEDBACK.md. Also reject mismatched contract addresses, reverted/missing receipts, forged or inconsistent provenance and replay data passed as live decisions. Assert validation never manufactures or mutates evidence.
- **Phase validation:** run relevant tests, typechecking and builds, plus the mandatory existing regressions, after every phase. Capture actual failures/skips. UI phases require screenshots and responsive/loading/error checks; documentation-only work does not imply live integration acceptance. Do not invent results, lower bounds or waive a failed test to meet the deadline.

## Out of Scope

- Changing WaveVault or WaveVaultDeployer, weakening allocation/turnover/loss/timing bounds, introducing a stale-exit reserve, or broadening the hook's approved pool without a separately justified and approved change.
- Unapproved deployments, contract replacements, fund transfers, account creation, paid services, new tools, private-key/seed/password handling or automatic worker-pin changes.
- Real yield guarantees, invented yield backing, dollar-peg guarantees, profits, safety, crash protection, production readiness, audits, DAO governance, cross-chain support, fully decentralized/trustless claims or ETF claims.
- Treating replay, Anvil, fixtures, dry runs, screenshot mockups or manual report forwarding as successful live Sepolia or CRE evidence.
- Automatically submitting feedback forms, recording a synthetic voiceover, selecting prizes or submitting the event application on the owner's behalf.
